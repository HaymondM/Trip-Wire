Plan Your Visit

The park is open daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Day-use parking is $8 per vehicle; annual passes are available at the visitor center.

Trail conditions

Ridge Loop and Creek Trail are open. Summit Trail is closed above the switchbacks for erosion repair.

Frequently asked questions

Dogs are welcome on leash on all trails except the boardwalk. Drones require a permit from the park office. Campfires are allowed only in designated rings, and firewood must be purchased locally to prevent the spread of invasive insects. Quiet hours run from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. in all campgrounds.
